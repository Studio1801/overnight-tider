Get it home and get it right

Keeping them alive

Live crabs and lobsters want cold and damp, not wet. Keep them in the fridge or a cooler with ice under a towel, never sitting in melted ice or tap water, and cook them the day you get them.

Cook only the ones that are still moving.

Steaming

Blue crabs
20 to 30 minutes, lid on, until bright orange
Lobster
around 12 to 15 min for a 1½ lb, until bright red
Shrimp
2 to 4 minutes, pull them as soon as they curl and turn pink

Yes, both. Tell us your ZIP when you send your order and we'll confirm the options, cost and day.

Can I buy a small amount, or is it bulk only?

Both. Two lobsters for dinner is as welcome as a case of shrimp for a restaurant.

Why don't you list every price?

Seafood prices move with the market and the season. Rather than post a number that's wrong by Friday, we confirm your price when you order. Specials are posted at the top of this page.

Fresh or frozen?

We carry both. Lobsters and crabs come live, shrimp come fresh head-on or as tails, and frozen is there when you want to stock up. Say which you want in your order notes.
